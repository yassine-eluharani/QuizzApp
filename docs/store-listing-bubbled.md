## Full description (max 4000)

Bubbled is a practice app for cloud and DevOps engineers. Fill in the bubbles on exam-style questions, learn why every answer is right or wrong, and rehearse the interview questions that actually come up.

CERTIFICATION PRACTICE
• AWS: Solutions Architect Associate, Developer Associate, SysOps Administrator, DevOps Engineer Professional
• Azure: AZ-900, AZ-104, AZ-305
• Google Cloud: Associate Cloud Engineer, Professional Cloud Architect, Professional Data Engineer
• DevOps: Terraform, Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA), Docker

INTERVIEW PREP
Six tool tracks: Ceph, Kubernetes, Docker, Linux, Ansible and Azure networking. Each has 12 Learn cards ordered from the basics to the hard parts, plus a 15-question quiz.

WHY BUBBLED
• 1,000+ practice questions
• An explanation for every answer, not just a score
• Bookmark the tricky ones and come back to them
• Scores, history and daily streaks so you can see yourself improve
• No account, no ads

FREE TO START
The sample quiz and Quiz 1 of every certification are free. More quizzes are on the way.
