# Privacy Policy — CloudPrep Quiz

**Effective date:** 2026-04-28
**Last updated:** 2026-04-28

## Who we are

CloudPrep Quiz ("the App") is a mobile application for cloud-certification exam preparation, published by **Yassine Eluharani** ("we", "us", "our").

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at **eluharaniyassine@gmail.com**.

## TL;DR

- The App stores your quiz history, bookmarks, streaks and settings **only on your device**. We never see them.
- If you buy CloudPrep Pro, **RevenueCat** and **Google Play** process the transaction. They receive purchase information; we do not receive your name, email, or payment details.
- We do **not** use third-party analytics, ad networks, or tracking SDKs.
- We do **not** sell or share your data.

## What data the App processes

### 1. Data stored locally on your device

The following data lives in your device's secure on-device storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain via `expo-secure-store`, plus Android `SharedPreferences` / iOS `NSUserDefaults` via `AsyncStorage`). It never leaves your device unless you choose to back up your device to your platform's cloud (Google One Backup, iCloud).

| Stored               | What it is                                                                    | Purpose                                      |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Quiz history         | List of quizzes you've taken, scores, dates                                   | Show your progress and stats                 |
| Bookmarks            | Question IDs you've saved                                                     | Power the Study tab                          |
| Streaks              | Daily study activity dates (last 365 days)                                    | Show your study streak                       |
| Onboarding flag      | A single boolean                                                              | Skip onboarding after the first launch       |
| Cached entitlement   | Whether your Pro purchase is active, and a hash of your anonymous purchase ID | Allow Pro access while offline               |
| Cryptographic secret | A random 32-byte key generated on first launch                                | Detect tampering with the cached entitlement |

You can erase all of this at any time by uninstalling the App or by clearing the App's storage in your device settings.

### 2. Data sent to third parties

We do not run our own user-account system. Some functionality relies on third parties who act as independent data controllers or processors. Each is documented below.

#### RevenueCat (in-app purchases)

When you open the paywall or complete a Pro purchase, the App calls the **RevenueCat** SDK and the RevenueCat Customer API. RevenueCat receives:

- An **anonymous user ID** generated by the SDK (a random UUID, not linked to your name or email)
- Your device's coarse country/region and OS/device model (for fraud detection and analytics on our purchase funnel)
- Purchase events (product ID purchased, transaction timestamp)
- Receipt data signed by Apple or Google to validate the purchase

RevenueCat is operated by RevenueCat, Inc. (USA). Their privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/.

You can request deletion of any data tied to your RevenueCat anonymous user ID by emailing **eluharaniyassine@gmail.com** with the user ID shown in the App's Profile screen.

#### Google Play Billing

When you complete a Pro purchase on Android, **Google Play** processes the transaction. Google receives the data necessary to charge your payment method and issues a receipt. We do not receive your payment details. See https://policies.google.com/privacy.

#### Apple App Store (iOS, future)

When the iOS version is published, **Apple** processes purchases on iOS. Apple receives transaction information; we do not receive your payment details. See https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

#### CloudPrep API (our backend)

For Pro users, the App fetches premium quiz questions from our backend API hosted at our servers. Each request includes:

- The **anonymous RevenueCat user ID** described above (so the backend can confirm your Pro entitlement)
- A request ID generated per request

The backend logs request metadata (timestamp, request ID, response status, IP address) for operational reasons (debugging, rate-limit enforcement, fraud detection). These logs are kept for **30 days**, after which they are deleted automatically.

We do not store request bodies, quiz answers, or any tie between your IP address and your purchase identity.

## Your rights (GDPR, CCPA, and similar laws)

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights:

- **Access** — request a copy of any personal data we or our processors hold about you.
- **Deletion** — request that we delete data tied to your anonymous user ID.
- **Rectification** — request that we correct inaccurate data.
- **Portability** — request your data in a machine-readable format.
- **Objection / restriction** — object to or restrict processing.
- **Withdraw consent** — where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time.
- **Lodge a complaint** with your local data-protection authority.

Because the App does not require a user account and we do not store identifying data on our servers, most rights can be exercised simply by uninstalling the App. For purchase-related data held by RevenueCat or by your app store, contact us at **eluharaniyassine@gmail.com** with the anonymous user ID shown in the Profile screen and we will route the request to the appropriate processor.

We respond to verifiable requests within **30 days**.

## Security

We protect your data with the following measures:

- All network traffic from the App uses TLS 1.2+ encryption.
- Sensitive on-device data is stored in `expo-secure-store`, which is backed by the Android Keystore and iOS Keychain.
- The cached Pro entitlement is signed with HMAC-SHA256 using a per-install secret so tampering can be detected.
- Our backend rejects unauthenticated admin actions and applies rate limits to all endpoints.
- Webhook events from RevenueCat are authenticated using a shared secret.

No system is perfectly secure. If you believe you have discovered a security issue, please report it to **eluharaniyassine@gmail.com**.
